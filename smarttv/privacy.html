Privacy Policy

Remote+

Remote+ (the “App”) does not collect any personal information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

For convenience, the App stores the following on your device only:

  • The name and network address of the last TV you connected to
  • The connection token issued by that TV

This information is never transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Network Access

The App accesses your local network to find and control TVs on the same Wi-Fi network. This communication stays entirely within your home network, and the App does not communicate with any external server.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

Because the App collects no information, there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The App contains no advertising and uses no analytics tools that track your activity.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
