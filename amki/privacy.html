Privacy Policy

Amki

Amki (the “App”) does not collect any personal information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account or sign-in is required.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device only:

  • The cards and decks you create
  • Your study and review history
  • Media files such as imported images and audio

This information is never transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Export and Import

For backup or moving to another device, you can export or import your data as an .amki file. This file is saved only to a location you choose, and the App never uploads it anywhere on its own.

4. Microphone Access

The App uses the microphone only when you record audio for a card. Recorded audio is stored on your device only and is not transmitted to any external server.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

Because the App collects no information, there is nothing to share with third parties.

6. Advertising and Analytics

The App contains no advertising and uses no analytics tools that track your activity.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
