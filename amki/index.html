Support

A local-first flashcard app you can use right away — no account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-up. All of your cards and study history stay on your device.

My cards are gone / I want to move my data

Amki keeps your data on your device only, so deleting the app also deletes your cards. To back up or move to a new device, go to Settings > Export to save an .amki file, then use Settings > Import on the other device to load it.

Can I import Anki decks?

Yes, Amki supports importing .apkg files. It extracts text, basic line breaks, images, and [sound:...] audio. Scripts and complex styling (HTML/JS) are not executed, so some formatting may appear simplified.

Audio recording isn't working

  • iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > turn on Amki.
  • Android: Settings > Apps > Amki > Permissions > allow Microphone.

My review schedule looks off

Amki uses a pluggable review scheduler. Your review history is stored in an append-only way that can't be modified or deleted, and the schedule is calculated from each card's review history. If the problem persists, email us and describe what happened.
