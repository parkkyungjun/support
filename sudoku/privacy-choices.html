Privacy Choices

Sudoku

Delete information stored on your device

Delete the App from your device to remove its locally stored puzzle progress and preferences. The App has no account and does not keep a server copy of this information.

Request deletion of purchase-related data

To request deletion of purchase-related data associated with the App’s RevenueCat project, email rudwns708.14564@gmail.com with the subject “Sudoku privacy request.” Do not send payment-card information. We may ask for a RevenueCat anonymous App User ID or transaction reference solely to locate the relevant record.

Use the App without purchasing

You can use every puzzle feature without making a purchase, and regular puzzle play works offline. When the App starts with a network connection, it may still contact RevenueCat to retrieve the supporter product’s price and availability.
