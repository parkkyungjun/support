How do I start a game?

On the home screen, choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert. A new puzzle starts immediately.

How do hints and notes work?

  • Select an empty cell and tap Hint to reveal the correct number.
  • Tap Notes to enter candidate numbers without committing to an answer.
  • Tap Erase to clear the selected cell.

Is an account or internet connection required?

No account is required, and regular puzzle play works offline. When a connection is available, the App may contact RevenueCat to check the price and availability of the optional supporter purchase.

How does the supporter purchase work?

Tap the character holding a Sudoku board at the bottom of the home screen to open the optional supporter page. The purchase does not unlock gameplay and is not required to use the App. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
