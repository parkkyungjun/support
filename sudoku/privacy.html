Privacy Policy

Sudoku · Effective August 16, 2026

Sudoku (the “App”) requires no account and contains no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics. Puzzle activity stays on your device. The App uses RevenueCat to check the price and availability of an optional supporter purchase and, if you buy it, to complete and validate the transaction.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores puzzle progress, completed-puzzle counts, interface preferences, supporter-star count, and a cached localized product price on your device. This information is used only to provide App functionality. The developer does not receive it, and it is removed when you delete the App or clear its local data.

2. Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The App does not require registration or sign-in. It contains no advertising SDK and no third-party behavioral analytics SDK. The developer does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, health data, or puzzle entries through the App.

3. Optional in-app purchase

When the App starts with a network connection, it may use RevenueCat to retrieve the current price and availability of the optional supporter purchase. If you make the purchase, the App also uses Apple’s in-app purchase system and RevenueCat to process and validate the transaction.

RevenueCat may process an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, device type and operating-system information, last-seen time, product and purchase history, locale and currency, and Apple receipt information. The App does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, phone number, advertising identifier, puzzle contents, or a custom account identifier.

Apple processes payment and App Store transaction information under Apple’s own privacy terms. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

4. Purpose and sharing

Purchase-related information is used only to display the available product, complete and validate the purchase, prevent transaction errors, and provide purchase support. It is shared only with Apple and RevenueCat as necessary for those purposes. It is not sold and is not used by the developer for advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Retention and deletion

On-device information remains until you delete the App or clear its local data. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records according to their legal, security, and service requirements. To request deletion of purchase-related data controlled through the developer’s RevenueCat account, follow the instructions on the Privacy Choices page.

6. Security and international processing

Purchase-related information is transmitted using encrypted connections. RevenueCat may process and store information in the United States and other locations where it or its service providers operate.

7. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional purchase is handled by Apple and is subject to the device owner’s App Store purchase settings and parental controls.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised policy and effective date will be posted on this page.
